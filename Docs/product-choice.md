# product-choice.md — Why we chose the L-Series

## Product Chosen
**NComputing L-Series (L250 / L300 / L350)**

Source: https://www.ncomputingindia.com/l-series/

## Why this product?

The L-Series was the easiest product in the NComputing lineup to understand and explain to a non-technical buyer.

The entire value proposition fits in one sentence:

> "One server PC can replace 30–100 full desktop PCs — at a fraction of the cost and power."

This maps naturally to a simple e-commerce flow:
- Pick the model (L250, L300, L350) based on resolution and port needs
- Pick quantity (how many desks you need to equip)
- Check out

Other NComputing products like vSpace Pro (software) or their enterprise VDI offerings require assisted sales, enterprise contracts, and IT infrastructure discussions — too complex for a self-serve ordering portal.

## Target Audience

Schools, coaching centers, and SMBs in India that need 20–100 workstations at once:

| Segment | Why they need L-Series |
|---|---|
| Computer labs (schools/coaching) | Need many identical low-use desktops, tight budget |
| Back office (SMBs, BPOs) | Data entry, billing counters — don't need full PCs |
| Government offices | High PC count, centralized IT management preferred |

## What they care about

1. **Upfront hardware cost** — PCs cost ₹40,000–₹80,000 each. L-series costs ₹5,000–₹8,000.
2. **Power bills** — A 40-seat PC lab draws ~5,000W. L-series draws ~160W for the same.
3. **IT maintenance** — Managing 40 individual PCs is painful. One server is easy.
